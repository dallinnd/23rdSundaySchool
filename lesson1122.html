“I Will Love Them Freely”

November 16 - 22, 2026

Doctrines in this weeks lesson

  • Covenants are a deeply personal, loving relationship (Hosea 1–3)
  • The Lord’s mercy is relentless, and He always invites us back (Hosea 2; 14; Joel 2:13)
  • True devotion must be inward, not just outward (Hosea 6:4–7; Joel 2:12–13)
  • The Lord pours out His Spirit to guide us in the latter days (Joel 2:28–32)
Rate the following questions on a scale from 1–5.

1 = Rarely
2 = Occasionally
3 = Sometimes
4 = Often
5 = Consistently

1. Do my spiritual habits feel like connection, not just routine?

2. Do I choose mercy over being "right"?

3. Am I trying to know God, not just check boxes?

4. Does my faith stay steady when life gets busy?

5. Am I the same person when no one is watching?

6. Do I put people's needs ahead of rigid rules?

7. Do I serve out of love rather than to be noticed?

8. When I mess up, do I want to change my heart, not just look sorry?

9. Do my private thoughts match my outward devotion?

10. Am I quick to soften my heart when prompted to change?

Class Discussion

1

How does viewing covenants as a loving relationship—rather than a contract—change daily habits like prayer or scripture study?

2

In Hosea 2:19–20, God promises we will "know the Lord." What helps you move from knowing about God to truly knowing Him?

3

When we feel like a 1 or 2, how does knowing God is "gracious and merciful" (Joel 2:13) make it easier to turn back to Him?

4

In Hosea 14:4, God promises, "I will heal their backsliding." Why does it matter that He heals our inconsistency instead of us fixing ourselves first?
