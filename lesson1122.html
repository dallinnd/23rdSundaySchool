“I Will Love Them Freely”

November 16 - 22, 2026

Doctrines in this weeks lesson

Rate the following questions on a scale from 1–5.

1 = Rarely
2 = Occasionally
3 = Sometimes
4 = Often
5 = Consistently

1. Do my spiritual habits feel like connection, not just routine?

2. Do I choose mercy over being "right"?

3. Am I trying to know God, not just check boxes?

4. Does my faith stay steady when life gets busy?

5. Am I the same person when no one is watching?

6. Do I put people's needs ahead of rigid rules?

7. Do I serve out of love rather than to be noticed?

8. When I mess up, do I want to change my heart, not just look sorry?

9. Do my private thoughts match my outward devotion?

10. Am I quick to soften my heart when prompted to change?
