"He Shall Direct Thy Paths"

September 7-14, 2026

Doctrines in this weeks readings

  • Fearing God means loving Him and willingly submitting our will to Jesus Christ
  • Trusting in the Lord over our Own Understanding
  • The Power of Communication and Kindness
  • Importance of Righteous Discipline and Christ-like Attributes
  • Finding Our Purpose Through Obedience
Reading and Scriptures
Q1

What have you learned about Jesus Christ this week?

Q2

How is fear of the Lord different from other kinds of fear?

Q3

With podcasts, influencers, music, parents, and friends all around us, how do you tell good counsel from noise?

Q4

Why is it unwise to lean on our own understanding? How have you discovered that the Lord is trustworthy?

Q5

What does it mean to let the Lord "direct [your] paths"? (Prov 3:6)

Q6

Tell about a time you look back on and can see He was directing your path — even if it didn't feel like it then.

Q6

When did the Savior exemplify Proverbs 15:1–4, 18? (see Mark 12:13–17; John 8:1–11)

Q7

As members of the Church of Jesus Christ, we have a unique understanding of God's plan. How has your understanding of God's Plan helped you keep an eternal perspective?
